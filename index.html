Nova Drift Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026

Nova Drift ("the game") is a small independent space game for Android and web browsers. This page explains what information the game uses and why. If you have a question, write to NovaDriftGame@gmail.com.

The short version

  • No accounts, no names, no chat. We never ask who you are.
  • Your scores, coins and settings stay on your own device.
  • The Android app shows an ad every few minutes of play, and you can choose to watch an ad for extra coins. All ads are set up for children: no personalized ads and no advertising ID.
  • We don't sell anyone's data.

1. What stays on your device

The game saves your best scores, settings, coins, ship upgrades and shop items, how long you have played since the last ad, and how many ads you watched for coins today. They are kept in the app's (or browser's) own storage on your device and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the browser's site data, deletes them.

2. Ads (Android app only)

The Android app shows one full-screen ad after every few minutes of play, between games, never while you play. You can also choose to watch an ad to double the coins from a game or to get extra coins in the shop; those are never shown unless you tap for them. The ads come from Google AdMob. The browser version has no ads.

  • Every ad request is marked as child-directed and as coming from someone under the age of consent, and is limited to ads suitable for all ages (rated G). Google then shows no personalized ads and doesn't build an advertising profile.
  • The app does not use the device's advertising ID.
  • To show an ad, Google still receives basic technical information, such as your IP address, device model, operating system and app version, and whether an ad was shown or tapped. Google uses it to deliver the ad, count views, and prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Playing online

1v1

If you play online 1v1, the two devices connect to each other using a random room code:

  • A connection service (PeerJS, at 0.peerjs.com) introduces the two devices to each other. The game's online code is downloaded from unpkg.com when you open 1v1.
  • The devices then exchange game data: ship positions, hits, gadgets and scores. No names or messages are sent.
  • When the devices connect directly, each one can see the other's IP address, as in any peer-to-peer game. When they can't, the game data goes through our relay server, which only passes it along. The relay may keep short technical logs (such as IP addresses and times) to keep it running and stop abuse; we don't use them for anything else.

Battle royale

If you play battle royale, your device connects to our match server in the European Union, which runs the match for up to 10 ships:

  • The server receives your device's IP address (every internet connection needs one) and, during the match, your game data: ship position, hits, gadgets and pickups. When the match ends, the game data is gone.
  • Every ship gets a random call sign for that match (like "CometFox42"). You never type a name, and there is no chat.
  • Other players never see your IP address: everything goes through our server.
  • The server keeps no record of who played. While you are connected it uses your IP address only to limit how many connections come from one address; it doesn't write IP addresses to its logs.

If you don't play online, none of this happens.

4. Fonts

The game loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the fonts are downloaded. See Google's privacy policy.

5. Children

Nova Drift is made for everyone, including children. The game doesn't ask for or collect children's personal information: there are no accounts, no names, no chat, and no way to share photos or contact details. The ads are set up for children as described above. If you are a parent and have a question or concern, write to NovaDriftGame@gmail.com and we'll help.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can play without going online, and the browser version has no ads.
  • We don't keep personal information about you (the battle royale server keeps none), apart from the 1v1 relay's short technical logs if you play online. You can ask us about them, or ask us to delete them, by email.
  • For the information Google receives for ads, see Google's privacy policy.

7. Changes

If the game starts using information differently, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the game.
